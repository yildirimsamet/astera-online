Commander field guide

How to Play Astera Online

Build a productive capital before you expose a valuable fleet. This guide follows the order a new commander can use during the first day.

For the complete current rules, costs and practical examples, open the Astera Online Wiki. The same Wiki is available from the in-game menu.

Your first game starts with 72 hours (3 days) of protection, once per new account. Existing accounts and later season joins receive 24 hours of protection. Other commanders cannot attack you during this period. Launching an attack against a player ends the shield early; mining, construction, research, scouting, and pirate combat do not.

  1. 1. Grow the Core and your mines

    Choose your active world and open Base → Production. Collect the Works into the Store before placing orders. Raise the Command Core when a needed local upgrade is blocked, then improve the producer for the resource you are missing. Deuterium Refinery upgrades also need Deuterium Synthesis research.

    Alloy pays for basic infrastructure and fleet. Crystal supports research and advanced technology. Deuterium pays for flight fuel as well as selected upgrades and research; retain enough to bring your next mission home.

  2. 2. Build a Prospector and mine asteroids

    Open Fleet → Yard and build a Prospector when you have room and resources. Select a discovered asteroid on the galaxy map and send it to mine. Another fleet may take the ore before you arrive. Returning ore enters the Works; collect it into the Store before spending it.

  3. 3. Develop the Hangar and Shipyard

    The Hangar determines fleet capacity and upgrades independently of the Command Core. The Shipyard unlocks ships and speeds ship and ground-defence construction. Check capacity, research requirements and resources before ordering a new ship.

  4. 4. Assemble a balanced first fleet

    Compare attack, durability, cargo, speed, price, and the Hangar space each ship uses. A force built only around attack may win a comparison and still return with losses. Cargo also matters because a fleet that cannot carry its possible loot leaves value behind.

  5. 5. Keep research moving

    Research unlocks systems and improves the economy and fleet. Some ships and actions require specific projects. Avoid spending every resource on a single research path: production, technology, and the working fleet support each other, and neglecting one makes the other two slower.

  6. 6. Challenge pirates with a plan

    Pirates are moving targets on the galaxy map. Surviving cargo ships can bring home loot after a successful battle. A decisive victory also has a chance to capture a ship. You can recall a pirate raid once before combat begins. It turns home without fighting or taking loot; spent fuel is not refunded. Compare the target, fuel and possible losses before sending.

  7. 7. Scout before fighting commanders

    Telescopes help you observe nearby space. To learn the specific resources and defences on another planet, select that world and send a probe. Information changes over time, so use a recent report and include travel time in the decision. Starting the attack ends your starting protection.

  8. 8. Colonise and defend deliberately

    Your capital’s Command Core opens colony slots at levels 9, 13 and 16. A decisive victory at a neutral world opens a colony race. Send the required Couriers, founding resources and fuel before that race closes. Winning a battle does not automatically grant ownership. See founding a colony for the full procedure.

    Each colony needs maintenance and defence. The Store protects a limited part of its resources. Aegis absorbs combat damage; ground defences add fighting units. Check every colony’s faults and loyalty before spending its repair budget.

A useful first-day rhythm: improve production, keep the Prospector active, start one valuable research project, inspect the map, and build only the fleet you can replace. Return regularly rather than leaving full storage and idle mission slots.
