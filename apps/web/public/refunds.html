Refund and Cancellation Policy

Astera Online offers optional planet skins. Each skin and the four-skin bundle is a one-time digital purchase for your game account. It is not a subscription and does not renew.

Who handles payment and refunds?

When Paddle Checkout is available, Paddle is the seller and handles payment, receipts, withdrawal requests and refunds. Paddle’s Refund Policy governs purchases processed by Paddle. This page explains how the process relates to Astera Online; it does not limit any statutory consumer rights.

How to request a refund or cancel an order

Use the support link in your Paddle receipt or visit paddle.net to find your transaction and request a refund or exercise an applicable withdrawal right. Include the purchase email and transaction or receipt number if you have them. Except where law requires a refund, purchases are generally non-refundable; submitting a request does not guarantee approval. Paddle may approve a refund at its discretion under its policy. A one-time skin purchase has no recurring billing to cancel.

Consumer withdrawal periods and exceptions vary by location and product use. For example, Paddle’s policy describes a 14-day withdrawal right for certain purchases in Türkiye, the EU, EEA, Switzerland and the UK, subject to the terms and exceptions stated there. Please consult Paddle’s current policy for the rules that apply to your transaction.

Delivery problems and access after a refund

If your paid skin is missing or does not work, email samety3503@gmail.com with your commander name and Paddle receipt or transaction ID. Never send card details or your password. We can investigate delivery; you can also request a refund through Paddle. Once a refund or chargeback is approved, access to the refunded skin or bundle ends. Any other skins you own remain available.

If Astera Online is permanently discontinued and a purchased skin can no longer be used, contact us and Paddle with your receipt. Refunds and other remedies will be assessed under Paddle’s policy and applicable statutory consumer rights; this policy does not exclude those rights.

Questions about the game or delivery? Contact Astera Online. For payment, cancellation or a refund request, use Paddle buyer support.
