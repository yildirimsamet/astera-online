## Kullanılan prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE original cinematic environmental artwork for ASTERA ONLINE, a mobile-first persistent multiplayer space strategy game. This is an ART-DIRECTION PREVIEW, no UI, no text, no labels, no logo. Wide landscape 3:2 composition. Scene: view from a sophisticated orbital industrial command deck and ship servicing bay, looking past authentic machined equipment onto the curved limb of a beautiful inhabited planet and a luminous deep starfield. ASTERA's established visual identity: realistic heavy gunmetal hardware, layered manufactured forms, graphite and brushed steel, black ceramic, precise restrained cold instrument lighting, some warm copper machining light, immense NASA-like space depth. The pictured subject is an environment, not a new named spaceship, no iconic invented hull in foreground. A distant dock structure and small indistinct hulls suggest scale. Lower and left forty percent is a quiet dark graphite foreground with physically textured surfaces and deep shadow, intentionally allowing tiny interface readouts to later sit in front without competition. Dominant scenic beauty and planetary rim right of center, warm industrial light upper-right, steel white and pale cold light toward the planet. Keep highlights well localized, rich photographic tonal range, depth through layered structure, premium polished hard-surface cinematic realism, hopeful epic mood. Do not flood the artwork in blue or cyan; warm natural materials and stars create variety while restrained teal pinlights recall Astera. Avoid fantasy ornament, neon outlines, cartoon illustration, magical crystals, medals, shields, logos, symbols, text, UI widgets, borders, frame, giant glowing sci-fi holograms, gratuitous smoke, explosions. This should feel like a sophisticated space game with tangible machinery and a live NASA photograph outside its window.
